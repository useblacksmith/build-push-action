# Contributor and agent instructions

## Customer privacy before publication

Before creating or updating public content, contributors and agents must anonymize
private customer context in PR titles, bodies, comments and reviews; branch names
and commit messages; and committed code, tests, fixtures and documentation. Never
copy private customer data from support threads, incidents, logs or connected
sources into this repository.

Remove customer names; organization, repository, domain, tenant and account
identifiers; identifying keys, paths and log excerpts; and private customer job,
support or incident links. Credentials, known tokens and secrets are private. Use
neutral wording, synthetic data and minimal reproducible examples.

Check screenshot and recording pixels, captions and filenames before publication.
Use synthetic reproductions or keep identifying evidence private. Never publish
first and scrub later.

Preserve this repository's public identity, dependencies and intentionally public
attributions, including public authors and third-party software. Do not add a real
customer-name blacklist.

If you find an inadvertent disclosure, stop propagating it and flag it privately
for authorized cleanup. Do not delete content or rewrite history without approval.
